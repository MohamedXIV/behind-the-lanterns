# Game Constitution — Behind the Lanterns
**Authority:** foundational design principles · **Version:** 0.1 · **Updated:** 2026-10-29

This document overrules illustrative examples in other GDDs. Amend principles deliberately; do not treat brainstorming as implementation authorization.

## Vision
A cozy folkloric open-world sandbox with danger, wonder, and everyday dignity. A free young person starts with modest work at a stable, builds a life, and dares to explore the unknown. Ordinary life and exceptional adventure strengthen one another.

**North star:** *The world does not exist to serve the player; the player exists within the world.*

## Locked pillars
1. **Sandbox, not a main-quest treadmill.** No mandatory central storyline, chosen-one plot, or imposed game ending. Long optional narrative arcs may have conclusions.
2. **Familiarity against wonder.** Real named lands and plausible trade, cities, livelihoods, and customs anchor an unrestricted, legendary world inspired by *One Thousand and One Nights*. No historical date, ruling dynasty, or exact timeline constrains creation.
3. **Discoverable world truth.** Places, secrets, dangers, objects, and opportunities exist regardless of the player's rumors, quest flags, or prior dialogue. Knowledge changes interpretation and choices; it does not instantiate truths.
4. **A modest life matters.** Jobs, friendships, lodging, crafts, farming, companions, and eventual home and boat ownership are valid play, never just preparation for combat.
5. **Curiosity and escape are core verbs.** Combat may be tactical and player-skill based against legitimate threats, but retreat, stealth, cleverness, and avoidance must be viable.
6. **Guards are not combat enemies.** Attacking city soldiers/guards/security causes scripted arrest and consequences; no ordinary guard combat, XP farming, or criminal power fantasy. The jail may offer social and narrative opportunities.
7. **No permanent wizard power climb.** The protagonist is ordinarily human; supernatural powers, if acquired, are temporary, conditional, or item-mediated, not a permanent magic superhero build.
8. **World rules are systemic.** Time, NPC schedules, travel, economy, ownership, law, and knowledge interact consistently. Author-designed discoveries and stories coexist with systemic simulation.
9. **Production by systems.** Build testable, content-independent capabilities with a tiny quantity of deliberately replaceable fixtures; do not let an accidental vertical-slice prototype harden into the product.
10. **Respect player time, money, attention.** No predatory monetization, energy restrictions, forced daily attendance, fear-of-missing-out pressure, or punitive mobile interruption design.
11. **Cozy is not childish or risk-free.** Warmth grows from contrast with mystery, unease, danger, and safe refuges. Never confuse cozy with a world without consequences.

## Locked presentation and platform direction
- **Engine:** Godot.
- **Art:** 2D detailed/high-resolution pixel-art direction, fixed elevated 3/4 top-down **oblique**, readable front facades; **not** true isometric or a shipping 2D/3D hybrid.
- **Primary target:** native mobile, landscape orientation, on-screen touch controls; desktop/Steam later.
- **Dialogue:** written/localizable text plus invented gibberish/click-like vocalizations rather than natural-language voice-over.
- **Player appearance customization:** deferred for initial releases to control art/animation cost.

## Economical ambition
- Gameplay should generate stories through interactions, not a forced flow of quests.
- Each new town, crop, item, relationship, rumor, or adventure should primarily be **content/configuration**, not a new engine.
- Prefer authored, meaningful places and encounters over procedurally generated filler.
- Systems should be extendable, but **never generic for its own sake**.

## Not game requirements
Illustrative examples are examples. Do not infer a fixed number of cities, romances, crops, NPCs, professions, quests, dungeons, weapon families, or travel encounters. Do not implement every suggested mechanic without a tracked decision.

## Interpretation
In case of conflict: explicit latest owner decision → Constitution → Decision Register → relevant GDD → example/prototype. Record any amendment in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
